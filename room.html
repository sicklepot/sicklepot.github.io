The Room

Have you been inside the room?

No.

Have you tried to enter it?

Yes.

How did you try?

I'm always noticing things around me, trying this and that. I came across the room by chance, and some ways of getting near it. I tried a few. None let me in.

Where is the room?

Wherever I am, the room is.

How big is the room?

Sometimes so small it can't be seen; sometimes so large it holds everything.

Why can't it be entered?

I don't know. Perhaps the room itself says so.

Has anyone ever entered it?

I don't know that either, though some claim to have.

What is inside the room?

I can't say.

How do you know there is such a room?

The room's existence and its inaccessibility are both unverifiable.

How do you know you haven't entered it?

You could just as well say we are all in the room, right now — but what would that mean? Like saying this apple is this apple - it says nothing at all.

In other words, the room exists only in the mind?

No. It does not exist in the mind; when it comes to entering the room, the mind is its opposite. No — it must be in the mind, if there is such a thing as the room.

So to enter the room, you would have to give up believing the room exists?

Yes.

Can the room be entered from the inside?

Closer.

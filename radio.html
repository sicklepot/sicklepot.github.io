奇美魔拉电台 Chimimora Radio

奇美魔拉是一个流动的艺术团体，关注去等级化的实践。我们组织艺术家驻留、展览、现场演出等活动。我们邀请大家把正在听或正在做的东西发给我们：田野录音、作曲、念白，或者只是碰巧录下的东西。奇美魔拉电台播出这些声音。 Chimimora is a nomadic art collective interested in non-hierarchical practices. We organise artist residencies, exhibitions, live performances and more. We invite people to send us what they are listening to or making: field recordings, compositions, spoken word or just something they happened to record. Chimimora Radio broadcasts these sounds.
